      The 'Supply Dashboard' page is opened

    action: |
      Click on 'Companies' filter
    expected: |
      The dropdown is displayed:

      -   the list of existing companies
      -   not active companies are displayed on the bottom of the list with “(Not Active)” before name
  - id: s-BZHMWDFH
    action: |
      Select any companies from the list
    expected: |

    expected: |

  - id: s-FENMYZ1F
    action: |
      Select any of the companies in the filtered list
    expected: |
      The selected item checkbox is checked. The Supply table is updated and only endpoints/inventories, that are connected to the selected companies are displayed
  - id: s-51B0HAPF
    action: |
      Input in the 'Search' field any symbols, that are not present in any company name

      -   No companies are displayed in the list
